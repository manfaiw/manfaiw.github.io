Last updated: August 25, 2026

This policy applies to apps and related services published under the name **Vantateer** and
operated by Man Fai Wong. A product may provide an additional privacy notice when its data
handling differs from this general policy.

## Information handled by our products

Depending on the product and features you choose, an app may process content you provide,
application settings, license or entitlement status, and technical information needed to operate
the product. Product interfaces identify features that connect to an online or third-party
service before those features are used.

Apps designed for local processing keep user content on the device unless you deliberately enable
an online feature, expose a local service to other devices, or send content through connected
third-party software. Local files remain under the control of the device account and can normally
be removed through the app or by uninstalling it.

## Purchases and distribution platforms

When an app is installed or purchased through an app store or another distributor, that platform
may process account, payment, tax, transaction, license, crash, and diagnostic information under
its own privacy policy. Vantateer does not receive complete payment-card numbers from a
distribution platform.

## Diagnostics and support

An app may create local logs needed for troubleshooting. Unless a product clearly provides and
describes an upload feature, Vantateer does not automatically receive prompts, documents, images,
audio, generated output, or local logs. If you contact support, information you choose to include
will be used to investigate and respond to the request.

Operating systems and distribution platforms may separately collect reliability or diagnostic
information according to their settings and policies. Vantateer may receive aggregated or
product-level reliability reports from those platforms.

## Sharing and sale of personal information

Vantateer does not sell personal information. Information is shared only when needed to provide a
feature you request, comply with law, protect users or services, or work with a service provider
described by the relevant product or platform.

## Retention and security

Information stored only on your device remains there until you remove it, uninstall the app, or
the operating system removes application data. Information voluntarily sent for support is kept
only as long as reasonably necessary to address the request and meet legal obligations. Reasonable
technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no storage or transmission method can be
guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children

Vantateer products are not directed to children under 13. If a product is intended for a different
audience, its product page will provide the relevant notice.
